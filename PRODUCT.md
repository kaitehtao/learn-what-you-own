# Learn What You Own product requirements

Learn What You Own is an evidence-led ownership intelligence platform. A user should be able to search a security, understand the underlying business, evaluate balanced bull and bear cases, inspect current and historical valuation, and trace material claims to primary evidence.

## Public product

- One canonical, versioned company dossier per issuer or security where share-class distinctions matter.
- Filed financial history, ownership, insider activity, risks, catalysts, business relationships, comparables, official releases, external journalism, interviews, and moderated research.
- Clear labels for verified facts, management statements, estimates, contributor opinions, and AI inference.
- Index pages are live views over one deduplicated universe; price, market capitalization, dates, membership, and ownership are sourced and time-stamped.

## Member product

- Private-by-default watchlists, portfolios, notebooks, attachments, messages, valuation work, earnings models, and thesis notes.
- Explicit publishing and moderation for community research.
- Alerts, contribution reputation, rewards, public profiles, and corrections without exposing private account data.

## Completion standard

A public route may be a clearly labeled starting page, but it is not a published research foundation until the active source gate passes. It is not benchmark complete until the active database rule also passes verified-claim, section, and evidence-class requirements. No UI label or hard-coded registry may override those database checks.

## Out of scope

The product does not execute trades, provide individualized investment advice, republish restricted research without rights, silently publish AI conclusions, or operate an unrestricted stock-chat feed.
