# Learn What You Own editorial standards

## Evidence hierarchy

1. SEC filings and other regulator or government records.
2. Official issuer investor-relations materials and direct management statements.
3. Licensed or publicly accessible independent journalism, industry data, and research.
4. Moderated contributor observations with identity, expertise, date, position, conflict, confidence, and evidence disclosures.

Management assertions are not independently verified facts. Estimates and inferences must be labeled and may not be presented as filed results.

## Publication

- Every material factual claim needs a real, accessible citation.
- SEC filings, official releases, and external coverage remain separate source classes.
- AI may extract, compare, and draft, but an editor approves thesis language and corrections.
- A research foundation requires at least five distinct sources. Benchmark completion additionally follows the active database quality rule.
- Earnings, 10-K, 10-Q, definitive proxy, and material 8-K detections are published automatically as evidence-preserving filing packages with exact EDGAR links. Automation may refresh filed facts but may not silently change bull, bear, valuation, or owner-summary conclusions; those interpretations remain separately moderated.

## Corrections and rights

Preserve correction history and superseded claims. Do not erase disagreement that remains evidence-supported. Do not host or summarize restricted third-party research without permission. Link or store metadata when full-text rights are unavailable.

## Safety

Research is educational, not individualized investment advice. Require position and conflict disclosures. Keep private research private unless the member explicitly submits it for moderation and publication.
